About

This is an informal weekly seminar run by Ph.D. students in graph theory at Yokohama National University. We share ongoing work, discuss research progress, and present our latest results in a friendly, collaborative setting. Our goals are to practice clear communication, exchange constructive feedback, foster collaborations, and generate new ideas to advance our research. We usually hold it at the following time and place.

  • Time: Thursdays, 3PM~ (JST)
  • Place: Room 611, S3-4 Building, Yokohama National University
